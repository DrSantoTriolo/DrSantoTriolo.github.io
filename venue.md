## Fritz Community Centre
*Baie-D'Urfé, Quebec*

We're thrilled to celebrate our special day at the beautiful Fritz Community Centre, located in the charming lakeside town of Baie-D'Urfé on Montreal's West Island. This historic and iconic venue offers the perfect blend of classic elegance and natural beauty for our wedding celebration.

### About the Venue

The Fritz Community Centre is one of the West Island's premier facilities for hosting weddings in a classic ambiance. Not only is it an iconic and historic landmark in the Town, but its location also provides a breathtaking view of Fritz Memorial Park and Lake St. Louis.

Our waterfront location and landscaped gardens offer the opportunity to host your wedding ceremony, picture taking and reception on site. The venue is surrounded by a large park with beautiful green spaces, making it perfect for both indoor and outdoor celebrations.

### Venue Details

**Address**: 20477 Lakeshore Road, Baie-D'Urfé, Québec H9X 1R3  
**Capacity**: 75 people (standing) / 55 people (seated)  
**Space**: 920 square feet (plus meeting room - 2nd floor 275 sq. ft.)  
**Contact**: 514-457-5324  

### What Makes This Venue Special

#### Waterfront Setting
Right by the water and the Lakeshore road, a perfect spot for nature lovers. The venue offers stunning views of Lake St. Louis, creating a romantic backdrop for our ceremony and photos.

#### Historic Charm
The Fritz Community Centre is a beloved historic landmark that has been serving the community for generations, adding character and significance to our celebration.

#### All-in-One Location
We love weddings with few locations since we don't need to scramble from one location to the next or worry about parking and traffic. Everything from getting ready to the ceremony and reception can happen on-site.

#### Beautiful Gardens
The landscaped gardens and surrounding park provide countless opportunities for beautiful wedding photography in a natural setting.

### Getting There

The venue is conveniently located on Lakeshore Road in Baie-D'Urfé, easily accessible from Montreal and surrounding areas. The waterfront location makes it a scenic drive for our guests.
