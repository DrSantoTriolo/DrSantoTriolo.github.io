## Projects stopped / paused / materially de-risked (or put at risk)

- **Oracle / OpenAI (Stargate infrastructure): Michigan data-center financing disrupted (risk of delay, but Oracle says timeline still on track).**
  - **What changed (Dec 17, 2025):** Oracle said its talks to secure an equity partner for a **>$10B, >1GW** data-center project in **Saline Township, Michigan** remain “on schedule,” but **will not include Blue Owl Capital** (previously in talks). Oracle/partner **Related Digital** said “full construction” is still expected to begin **Q1 2026**, but Reuters notes Oracle **had not signed a deal with a replacement backer** at the time of publication. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/oracles-10-billion-michigan-data-center-limbo-after-blue-owl-funding-talks-stall-2025-12-17/))
  - **Why it matters for frontier AI:** This site is described as part of **Oracle + OpenAI’s Stargate AI infrastructure push**—i.e., a direct compute bottleneck risk for frontier training/inference plans. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/oracles-10-billion-michigan-data-center-limbo-after-blue-owl-funding-talks-stall-2025-12-17/))

## Notable hires, promotions, and leadership exits (frontier-model orgs + critical suppliers)

- **OpenAI: hired former UK Chancellor George Osborne to lead “OpenAI for Countries” (Stargate global expansion).**
  - **Decision (announced Dec 16, 2025):** OpenAI appointed **George Osborne** as **Managing Director and Head of “OpenAI for Countries”**, starting **January 2026**, to lead overseas expansion tied to the **$500B “Stargate”** data-center initiative. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-taps-former-uk-finance-minister-osborne-lead-global-stargate-expansion-2025-12-16/))
  - **What this signals:** an explicit bet on **state-level partnerships** (sovereign compute, national AI strategies) as a scaling path for frontier deployment and infrastructure access. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-taps-former-uk-finance-minister-osborne-lead-global-stargate-expansion-2025-12-16/))

- **Amazon (AWS/AGI org): major AI org reshuffle; senior exit; frontier-model research lead named.**
  - **Decision (reported Dec 17, 2025):**
    - **Rohit Prasad** (key Alexa/AI exec) will **step down at year-end (Dec 31, 2025)**.
    - **Peter DeSantis** will lead a new unit unifying **Amazon’s Nova models**, **custom silicon (Graviton/Trainium)**, and **quantum** efforts.
    - **Pieter Abbeel** is set to **head “frontier model research”** within Amazon’s AGI organization (and continue robotics work). ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/amazon-taps-veteran-peter-desantis-lead-ai-chip-quantum-push-2025-12-17/))
  - **Why it matters:** Amazon is explicitly bundling **models + chips + infra** under one leader—an organizational design aimed at tighter feedback loops between frontier training, inference cost, and silicon roadmaps. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/amazon-taps-veteran-peter-desantis-lead-ai-chip-quantum-push-2025-12-17/))

- **Meta: Yann LeCun leaving to found “world models” startup AMI Labs (major strategic talent shift + externalized frontier bet).**
  - **Decision/new confirmation (Dec 18–19, 2025 reporting):**
    - Multiple outlets report LeCun is leaving Meta **by end of 2025** and fundraising for **Advanced Machine Intelligence (AMI) Labs** (often described as focusing on **“world models”**—systems built to understand the physical world from rich visual/spatiotemporal data). ([techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/19/yann-lecun-confirms-his-new-world-model-startup-reportedly-seeks-5b-valuation/?utm_source=openai))
    - Reported fundraising discussions: **~€500M at ~€3B valuation** (early-stage; terms may change). ([sifted.eu](https://sifted.eu/articles/yann-lecun-ami-labs-3bn-valuation/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Why it matters:** this is a high-profile shift away from an incumbent frontier lab into an “outside-the-org” vehicle—often a sign that a major research direction (here: post-LLM architectures) may be better pursued with **independent governance + capitalization** than inside a product-driven hyperscaler. ([techcrunch.com](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/19/yann-lecun-confirms-his-new-world-model-startup-reportedly-seeks-5b-valuation/?utm_source=openai))

## Other high-signal “frontier capability” decisions (compute, funding, and platform leverage)

- **SoftBank: scrambling to complete a $22.5B OpenAI funding commitment by end of 2025.**
  - **New info (Reuters, published Dec 20, 2025 UTC / datelined Dec 19):** SoftBank is raising funds (including selling stakes like Nvidia/T‑Mobile) to fulfill a **$22.5B** OpenAI commitment by **Dec 31, 2025**, per sources—illustrating the capital intensity (and fragility) of frontier-scale compute plans. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/softbank-races-fulfill-225-billion-funding-commitment-openai-by-year-end-sources-2025-12-19/))

- **Google Cloud + Palo Alto Networks: expanded partnership “approaching $10B,” with new AI-driven security services.**
  - **New info (Dec 19, 2025):** Reuters reports a multi-year commitment “approaching **$10B**,” including migration + development of **AI-involved security services**, highlighting that frontier-model orgs are also competing on **security/compliance wrappers** as AI-driven attack/defense escalates. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/google-cloud-lands-deal-with-palo-alto-networks-approaching-10-billion-per-2025-12-19/))

- **Nvidia: FTC cleared Nvidia’s $5B investment in Intel (supply-chain/compute industrial base move).**
  - **New info (Dec 19, 2025):** U.S. antitrust agencies cleared Nvidia’s previously announced **$5B** Intel investment, which could influence future **advanced packaging/foundry capacity** and the geopolitics of AI compute supply. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/nvidia-intel-deal-cleared-by-us-antitrust-agencies-2025-12-19/))
