  - **Acquired company focus / approach**

  - **Acquired company focus / approach**
    - Neptune is an **experiment tracking + training monitoring** system used for **GPT-scale training**, oriented around high-cardinality metrics (including **per-layer** metrics) and fast visualization/debugging at scale. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-neptune/?utm_source=openai))

    - OpenAI states it plans to integrate Neptune **deep into its training stack** to expand visibility into how models learn. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-neptune/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Sources:** OpenAI announcement ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-neptune/?utm_source=openai)); Neptune product positioning ([neptune.ai](https://neptune.ai/product?utm_source=openai)); Reuters report on the deal ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-agrees-acquire-ai-startup-neptune-boost-model-training-capabilities-2025-12-04/?utm_source=openai))
