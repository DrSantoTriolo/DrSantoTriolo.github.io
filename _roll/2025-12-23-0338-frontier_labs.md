## Acquisitions (focus, approach, competitive advantages, unique details)
- **Alphabet (Google) to acquire Intersect Power for $4.75B (announced Dec 22, 2025) — acquisition is explicitly framed around powering AI/datacenter growth**
  - **Decision:** Alphabet announced it will acquire **Intersect Power** for **$4.75B (cash + assumed debt)**. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/alphabet-buy-data-center-infrastructure-firm-intersect-475-billion-deal-2025-12-22/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Acquired company focus (what it does):**
    - Intersect is described as a **clean energy / data-center energy developer** building power infrastructure aligned with datacenter needs. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/alphabet-buy-data-center-infrastructure-firm-intersect-475-billion-deal-2025-12-22/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Approach (how it operates):**
    - Emphasis on **developing renewable power + energy infrastructure** and (per Reuters) exploring **industrial parks co-locating datacenters with clean-energy plants**. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/alphabet-buy-data-center-infrastructure-firm-intersect-475-billion-deal-2025-12-22/?utm_source=openai))
    - Reuters also notes a **“Quantum” energy storage system** being built next to a Google datacenter in Texas (mentioned as remaining with Intersect’s existing investors). ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/alphabet-buy-data-center-infrastructure-firm-intersect-475-billion-deal-2025-12-22/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Competitive advantages / “why this target”:**
    - **Scale/pipeline:** Reuters reports Intersect brings **$15B in assets operational or under construction** and projects expected to generate **10.8 GW by 2028** (positioning it as a large, ready pipeline in a tight power market). ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/alphabet-buy-data-center-infrastructure-firm-intersect-475-billion-deal-2025-12-22/?utm_source=openai))
    - **Strategic control over a bottleneck:** The deal is a vertical move into **power sourcing/development**, a core constraint for frontier-model training/inference expansion. (Inference based on Alphabet’s stated AI power needs and the nature of the asset.) ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/alphabet-buy-data-center-infrastructure-firm-intersect-475-billion-deal-2025-12-22/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Unique deal structure / details to note:**
    - Reuters: Alphabet’s acquisition includes Intersect’s **energy and datacenter projects in development/under construction**, while **some operational assets in Texas and California remain separate** with existing investors. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/alphabet-buy-data-center-infrastructure-firm-intersect-475-billion-deal-2025-12-22/?utm_source=openai))
    - AP: Intersect is expected to **continue operating independently** after close; close targeted for **first half of next year**. ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/e1eda1fb34b345e4e92ee0010be59f71?utm_source=openai))
