## Notable hires / leadership moves (frontier-model orgs)

- **OpenAI — hired Denise Dresser (Slack CEO) as its first “Chief Revenue Officer” (announced Dec 9, 2025)**
  - **Decision:** build a senior revenue org focused on enterprise sales + customer success; Dresser reportedly reports to COO Brad Lightcap. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-taps-slack-ceo-denise-dresser-chief-revenue-officer-2025-12-09/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Why it matters (inference):** suggests OpenAI is formalizing a profit/enterprise operating model (revenue ownership at C-level), likely to scale distribution for frontier-model products (ChatGPT / API / enterprise offerings). ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-taps-slack-ceo-denise-dresser-chief-revenue-officer-2025-12-09/?utm_source=openai))

- **Google — appointed Amin Vahdat as “chief technologist for AI infrastructure” (reported Dec 10, 2025)**
  - **Decision:** elevate AI infrastructure leadership as capex ramps; Reuters cites an internal memo via Semafor. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/google-names-amin-vahdat-new-chief-ai-infrastructure-buildout-semafor-reports-2025-12-10/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Competitive angle:** reinforces Google’s bet that **compute + systems execution** (including Google’s in-house TPUs) is a key differentiator in frontier-model competition. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/google-names-amin-vahdat-new-chief-ai-infrastructure-buildout-semafor-reports-2025-12-10/?utm_source=openai))

## Acquisitions & strategic investments (Dec 8–15 only)

- **OpenAI × Disney — $1B equity investment + 3-year IP licensing agreement for Sora and ChatGPT Images (announced Dec 11, 2025)**
  - **Decision:** Disney becomes the **first major content licensing partner** for Sora; Disney also becomes a major OpenAI API customer; curated Sora fan videos planned for Disney+. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/disney-sora-agreement/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Focus / approach:**
    - Allow Sora to generate short fan videos using **200+ Disney/Marvel/Pixar/Star Wars** characters (plus related props/vehicles/environments).
    - Explicitly excludes **talent likenesses/voices**. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/disney-sora-agreement/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Competitive advantages / unique details:**
    - **For OpenAI:** a rare, large-scale **licensed IP moat** for consumer video generation (reduces legal exposure vs “unlicensed character” prompting; could boost mainstream adoption of Sora). ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/disney-sora-agreement/?utm_source=openai))
    - **For Disney:** new distribution channel for IP engagement; potential internal productivity/tooling via OpenAI APIs + ChatGPT for employees. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/disney-sora-agreement/?utm_source=openai))
    - Deal includes **warrants** for Disney to purchase additional OpenAI equity. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/disney-sora-agreement/?utm_source=openai))

## Other frontier-model “decisions” worth tracking (strategy / policy / deployment)

- **OpenAI — launched GPT-5.2 after an internal “code red” acceleration push (reported Dec 11, 2025)**
  - Reuters describes GPT-5.2 variants (Instant / Thinking / Pro) and rollout to paid ChatGPT plans. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/openai-launches-gpt-52-ai-model-with-improved-capabilities-2025-12-11/?utm_source=openai))
  - This is less about “stopping” projects, more about **accelerating roadmap execution** under competitive pressure (Gemini). ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/openai-launches-gpt-52-ai-model-with-improved-capabilities-2025-12-11/?utm_source=openai))

- **OpenAI — changed equity compensation to remove the 6-month “vesting cliff” (reported Dec 13, 2025)**
  - **Decision:** new hires begin vesting immediately (talent-competition move). ([wsj.com](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openai-ends-vesting-cliff-for-new-employees-in-compensation-policy-change-d4c4c2cd?utm_source=openai))

- **Meta — reportedly considering charging for access to a future frontier model (“Avocado”)**
  - **Decision under consideration:** move away from a strictly “open(-ish)” release posture toward **monetized / more proprietary access** for top-end models. ([theverge.com](https://www.theverge.com/news/841809/meta-avocado-ai-model-charge-open-source-mark-zuckerberg?utm_source=openai))

- **Google DeepMind + UK government — expanded partnership on AI security + science (Dec 11, 2025)**
  - **Decision:** MoU includes priority access for UK researchers to DeepMind “AI for Science” tools and deepened work with the UK AI Security Institute (AISI), plus a planned automated research lab. ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ai-to-accelerate-national-renewal-and-growth-as-google-deepmind-backs-uk-tech-and-science-sectors?utm_source=openai))

## Appendix — Just outside the strict 7-day window (2025-12-01 → 2025-12-07), but high-signal

- **OpenAI → acquisition: Neptune (announced Dec 3, 2025)**
  - **What Neptune is (focus):** experiment tracking + training observability for foundation-model training (monitoring/debugging runs at scale). ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-neptune/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Approach / competitive advantage:**
    - Designed for **scalable** logging/querying of training metadata (packages for logging + querying; web UI for comparing large volumes of runs/metrics). ([docs.neptune.ai](https://docs.neptune.ai/3.20250901/about?utm_source=openai))
    - OpenAI explicitly frames this as gaining “deeper insight into how frontier models learn,” and integrating tooling “deep into our training stack.” ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-neptune/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Deal color:** Reuters reported OpenAI paying **< $400M in stock** (not confirmed by OpenAI), and noted OpenAI already used Neptune to monitor/debug GPT training. ([finance.yahoo.com](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/openai-agrees-acquire-ai-startup-001431177.html?utm_source=openai))

- **Anthropic → acquisition: Bun (announced Dec 3, 2025)**
  - **What Bun is (focus):** high-performance JavaScript runtime + all-in-one toolkit (runtime/package manager/bundler/test runner). ([anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-bun-as-claude-code-reaches-usd1b-milestone?utm_source=openai))
  - **Approach / competitive advantage:** Anthropic positions Bun as **speed + developer-experience infrastructure** that compounds Claude Code’s agentic coding momentum; Bun remains **open-source (MIT)** per announcement. ([anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-bun-as-claude-code-reaches-usd1b-milestone?utm_source=openai))

- **Meta → acquisition: Limitless (announced Dec 5, 2025)**
  - **What Limitless is (focus):** AI pendant + companion app that records/transcribes/summarizes real-world conversations (memory augmentation). ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-acquires-ai-wearables-startup-limitless-2025-12-05/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Project stoppage:** Limitless said it would **stop selling devices to new customers** (support existing users for a period). ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-acquires-ai-wearables-startup-limitless-2025-12-05/?utm_source=openai))
  - **Competitive advantage (why Meta buys this):**
    - Teams + know-how for **always-on audio capture + summarization UX**, likely complementary to Meta’s AI wearables roadmap (e.g., smart glasses). ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-acquires-ai-wearables-startup-limitless-2025-12-05/?utm_source=openai))
